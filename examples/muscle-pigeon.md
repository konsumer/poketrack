Often it helps to work out the basic structure in some notes like  this, and then you can tweak it a bit, once you get it layed down. Without knowing exactly what would go in each part, I mapped out the vibe first, then setup some rudimentary instruments (using built-ins) and hammered it all out. After that, I completely changed the instruments (maintaining the original purpose, but the sound was totally different.)

Essentially, it helps me to "prototype" the beat/melodies/etc first, with whatever instruments are simple/easy/fast, then tune the sound of the instruments and effetcs in another step to make it sound good.

Obviously, everyone's process is different, but this generally how I make songs.
